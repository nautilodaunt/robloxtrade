Mass send trades

Sends your offer to people whose recent Rolimon’s ads give what you want for it, and keeps watching for new ads. At most one trade per minute.

Hold Ctrl (Windows) or ⌘ Command (Mac) to pick several. Sorted by value. One item per box; repeat an item to ask for two copies. Leave unused boxes blank.
A tag only counts when your offer really fits it (e.g. Upgrade = they get fewer items, Demand = all your items are High/Amazing demand). Robux and Wishlist requests are never matched.
Zero Robux on both sides; only your selected copies. Blocks sending if what you get is worth less than what you give (Rolimon’s value, RAP if unvalued) or is projected. People who can’t trade or no longer have the items are skipped. Pauses on rate limits, verification or uncertain results. Each person gets the same trade at most once. Stop can’t cancel sent trades; manage those in Roblox Outbound. Mass trading can get an account limited, so use it carefully.
